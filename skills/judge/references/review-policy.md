# Judgment independence and closure v1.0.0

Contract ID: agentic-development/review-policy
Contract version: 1.0.0

## Plan-time choice

Before implementation, record required reviewer independence, acceptance scope,
closure authority and verification proportional to risk. Default: independent
review is required for US acceptance and release recommendations. A current user
or active trusted project policy may explicitly waive independence for a defined
small/local slice; record that source and scope. A builder's saved plan or an
absence of reviewer tools is not a waiver. Do not widen the slice or lower its
mandatory evidence to make it easier to close.

A narrow authoring/test task can complete under its own documented quality gates
without claiming parent US acceptance or release approval. Mark its self-review
as limited and preserve open independent/real validation tasks. This is not a
waiver of an existing task criterion requiring independent review.

## Declare independence dimensions

Record reviewer identity/context, participation in construction, inherited
context, exposure to builder conclusions or expected verdict, direct access to
frozen candidate and raw evidence, and write permissions. Different session IDs
or different models alone do not prove independence. Give a reviewer criteria,
necessary factual context, candidate and evidence; do not inject the builder's
private reasoning or desired verdict. Exposure to builder conclusions must be
disclosed as a limit. Reviewers must not repair or mutate the candidate.

## Verdict and closure gates

If independence is required and unavailable, use indeterminate unless a known
blocking defect already establishes needs-correction. Full tests or a high score
do not compensate. If an explicit scoped waiver permits limited self-review,
label the verdict limited, cite the waiver and keep broader US/release gates open.
No US/parent issue closure from self-review without a current explicit grant
covering that closure, and never contrary to a required independence gate.

Acceptance needs all mandatory scope criteria evidenced for the exact candidate
with no blockers. Score is separate and cannot authorize closure. A tracker
update is a separate permitted action: verify authority and actual outcome.
Only the designated owner closes the completed issue's scope; do not close a
shared parent because one subtask finished. Human approval is never self-granted.
