# Research readiness review

Use before declaring research sufficient for a particular planning decision. Reuse
current cited context and the caller's investigation/evidence contracts. The goal
is enough grounded understanding for an executable plan, not exhaustive research
or a new procedure/ticket for every source.

## 1. Explain the decision chain

Keep a compact, cited chain for each decision-material conclusion:
**hypothesis → evidence → supported premise → decision → tasks → observable criteria**.
A hypothesis is a question to test, not a premise until supported. Evidence includes
contrary findings and its scope/version/environment limits; a supported premise
states only what that evidence warrants. Label conclusions observed, inferred or
unverified; an inference cites its premises, not merely its confidence. Decisions
and tasks may be proposed planning inputs, not fabricated approved tickets.

For a remaining assumption record its owner (or unknown), affected decision/tickets,
readiness impact and smallest deciding probe with an observable resolving result.
Absence of a search result is not evidence that a problem or capability is absent.
Do not promote library documentation, generic design patterns or a familiar request
into proof of actual local behavior.

## 2. Ask mixed sufficiency questions

For each open inquiry explain the answer using targeted citations. Answer its closed
question **yes / no / unknown**: yes passes, no fails, unknown never passes. Inactive
questions may be N/A with a scope-based reason. No mandatory matrix or blank catalog.

| Open inquiry | Closed sufficiency question |
|---|---|
| What problem does the actual user, maintainer or system encounter, and which outcome matters now? | Are purpose, requested outcome, current behavior and boundaries grounded in accessible primary sources without invented users/capabilities? |
| Which hypothesis explains the issue or supports the option, and what would refute it? | Is each decision-critical premise supported by applicable evidence, with observed/inferred/unverified status and contradictions exposed? |
| Where will the decision have impact, and what sources must a worker read to act safely? | Are relevant actual files/symbols/tests, consumers and preserve/exclude boundaries identified with citations, rather than guessed paths or broad folders? |
| Which known functional, code, library or domain-principle problems could invalidate the approach here? | Have relevant local known issues, actual code/tests, dependency limitations and applicable principles been checked, or material unknowns assigned an owner and deciding probe? |
| What changes between the cited source/library version and this environment? | Are source identity, library version, environment and freshness recorded when material, with unavailable/stale inputs explicit? |
| Why choose this option over the smallest viable alternative, and what must happen before implementation? | Can supported premises justify the decision, task prerequisites and independent observable criteria without an unsupported leap? |
| What information would actually change the next plan, and what can proceed without it? | Are critical gaps scoped to affected decisions/tickets, nonblocking assumptions explicit and the next resolving action executable within authority? |

Inspect only consumed, relevant sources; pass ordered file/section/symbol pointers
with reasons and identity to Plan under the existing pointer contract. This is not
a mandate to load all catalogs, patterns, tests or domain principles. A source check
is static evidence, not reproduced behavior; for a bug retain the caller's
symptom-specific reproduction gate or precise missing fixture/access.

## 3. Resolve material gaps or stop

Start with relevant local evidence. Search external known issues only when risk or
uncertainty could change the plan, the question is bounded and an authorized channel
is available. Record source/version/date/applicability and limits; do not infer a
negative fact from unavailable searches. No implicit paid web access, installation,
model calls, exhaustive web review, full tests or evaluation campaign. Stable,
sufficient local evidence needs no external refresh ritual.

Stop when every applicable question is answered or inactive with justification and
the cited chain supports the next decision. Critical gaps block only affected
planning/implementation tickets, not the rest of the ready frontier. Return each
gap's owner, impact and deciding probe rather than claiming sufficiency or creating
a speculative backlog. Nonblocking assumptions travel with the planning inputs.
No performance/agent-effectiveness claim or acceptance follows from this review.
